01UI/UX DESIGN · MOBILE APP

GreenPlan

A digital experience designed to encourage greener everyday habits through a clear and approachable mobile interface.

Overview

GreenPlan explores how a mobile experience can make greener everyday choices feel more approachable. The project focuses on presenting the idea in a clear, friendly way so that sustainability feels easier to understand and engage with.

Design focus

The interface and visual presentation are designed to make the concept feel organised and accessible. The project combines a cover visual with mobile-screen designs to communicate the experience across the overall concept and a phone-sized interface.

Project intent

The goal is to support more mindful everyday habits through a digital product concept. The emphasis is on clarity, approachability, and presenting the experience in a way that users can understand quickly.

02WEB APP · PRODUCT DESIGN

RP Helper

A helper tool concept designed to make common student tasks and information easier to access.

Overview

RP Helper is a student-focused digital helper concept. It explores how a single, approachable experience could make useful student information and common tasks easier to find, rather than making users search across multiple places.

Design focus

The project is presented as a practical digital product, with attention to making the purpose easy to understand and the interface straightforward to navigate. Its supporting demos can be used to show the intended flow and how the concept works across screens.

Project intent

The aim is to reduce friction when students need help with everyday school-related tasks. This portfolio entry can be expanded with the exact features, tools, personal contribution, and lessons learned from the final project.
