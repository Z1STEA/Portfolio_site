OVERVIEW

Making school information easier to access.

Students can spend time searching across different RP pages for information about exams, timetables, policies and other school questions. RP Helpert was created to give students a faster conversational way to find answers.

The project uses Botpress with a knowledge base containing 88 FAQs across Admissions, Courses, Fees, Scholarships, Student Life, Campus Facilities and Contact information.
